# Personal installation instead of a per-project one

## TLDR

Let one person install OpenAgent for themselves, without adding it to a project's repository. The requester wants to use some of OA's skills on Vike (for example, account rotation) without pushing OA onto the project's other contributors. Today OA installs per project, so its skills and files get committed where every contributor sees them. Ideas are open: the issue asks for them.

## Why it matters

People who don't own a project, or who don't want to impose OA on everyone else working on it, can't use OA there. A personal installation lets one contributor adopt OA on a shared project without the others having to agree.
